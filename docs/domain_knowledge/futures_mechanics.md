# B3 Futures Mechanics & The Rollover Problem

To engineer robust Machine Learning pipelines for Quantitative Finance, especially in the Brazilian Market (B3), understanding the mechanics of Futures Contracts is a fundamental prerequisite. The architecture described in this repository (specifically the Silver Layer's `Backward Difference Splicing`) exists to solve the mathematical anomalies generated by these mechanics.

## 1. What are Futures Contracts?
Unlike a stock (e.g., PETR4) that is held in perpetuity, a Futures Contract is an agreement to buy/sell an underlying asset (Index or Dollar) at a **specific future date**. 
Because they have an expiration date, when the deadline arrives, the contract settles, and the market's liquidity migrates entirely to the contract of the subsequent period. The transition of liquidity from the old contract to the new one is called a **Rollover**.

## 2. Expiration Codes (The Letter Soup)
A contract code at B3 consists of 3 parts: **[Asset] + [Expiration Month] + [Year]**.
Months are represented by specific letters (international standard):

| Letter | Month    | Letter | Month     |
| :----: | :------- | :----: | :-------- |
| **F**  | January  | **N**  | July      |
| **G**  | February | **Q**  | August    |
| **H**  | March    | **U**  | September |
| **J**  | April    | **V**  | October   |
| **K**  | May      | **X**  | November  |
| **M**  | June     | **Z**  | December  |

*(Letters that could cause visual confusion, like I, L, and O, are skipped).*

## 3. Mini Bovespa Index (WIN)
The Mini Index represents fractions of the Ibovespa. It expires every **2 months** (always on even months).
- **Expirations:** Feb (G), Apr (J), Jun (M), Aug (Q), Oct (V), Dec (Z).
- **Exact Date:** Wednesday closest to the 15th of the expiration month.
- **Example:** In February 2026, the market trades `WING26`. As the expiration Wednesday approaches, institutional trading algorithms "roll over" their positions by selling `WING26` and buying `WINJ26` (April).

## 4. Mini US Dollar (WDO)
The Mini Dollar represents the BRL/USD exchange rate. It has a **monthly** expiration (higher rotation).
- **Expirations:** Every month (all letters from the table above).
- **Exact Date:** The first business day of the expiration month.
- **Example:** `WDOU26` expires on the 1st business day of September. In the last 2-3 days of August, liquidity heavily migrates to the October contract (`WDOV26`).

## 5. The Rollover Problem (The ML "Gap")
Every futures contract prices in the "Cost of Carry" (interest rate for the period). The Dollar contract expiring in 1 month has that month's local interest rate (Selic) embedded in it, compared to the US interest rate.

Because of this, **the price of the new contract is NEVER equal to the price of the old contract on the day of the rollover**.

### The Illusion of a Jump
* `WING26` closes at 130,000 points.
* `WINJ26` (the new active contract) opens the next day at 131,200 points (due to embedded interest rates).

If a Machine Learning model (like our Deep Reinforcement Learning network) sees this raw historical series, it will assume the market rallied 1,200 points overnight. It will learn false patterns and noise, severely damaging the Alpha generation.

## 6. The MLOps Solution: Backward Difference Splicing
To solve this, our **Silver Layer** data pipeline does not feed raw B3 data to the models. We calculate the exact transition Gap (e.g., 1,200 pts) and subtract it retroactively from the entire historical series of `WING26`. 

This cumulative backward shift ensures that the time series becomes perfectly continuous. Percentage returns and historical volatilities are mathematically preserved, allowing the neural network to train on the true variance of the market without being polluted by artificial rollovers.
